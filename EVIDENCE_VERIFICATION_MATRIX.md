**Standard Audit Classifications (Strictly 1 Primary Classification per Claim):**
1. `VERIFIED_OFFICIAL_DOCUMENTATION`: Explicitly documented in official Canon service documentation, parts catalogs, or technical manuals.
2. `VERIFIED_BINARY_ANALYSIS`: Confirmed by disassembly, PE export/import inspection, or string analysis of authentic Canon binaries (`reference/drivers/`).
3. `VERIFIED_PHYSICAL_HARDWARE`: Confirmed by real-world physical USB transactions, electromechanical observation, or sensor actuation on the connected printer.
4. `VERIFIED_USB_CAPTURE`: Confirmed via USB bus packet captures or low-level WinUSB transfer traces.
5. `VERIFIED_SOFTWARE_TEST`: Empirically validated via automated regression tests or test harnesses in the codebase.
6. `INFERRED`: Derived logically from engineering constraints, shared platform lineage, or observable patterns, but lacking direct silicon confirmation.
7. `SPECULATIVE`: Plausible working hypothesis lacking direct documentation, binary, or hardware confirmation.
8. `UNKNOWN`: Insufficient evidence to establish ground truth.
9. `DISPROVEN`: Previously asserted claim proven incorrect by forensic evidence or empirical test.
